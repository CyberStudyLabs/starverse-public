Privacy

Updated November 24, 2026

Starverse Learning, a product of NovaCore Technologies LLC, doesn’t collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no sign-ins, no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics.

What stays on your device

Starverse works entirely in your web browser.

  • Classroom mode (the default): progress is kept only while the browser tab is open. It is cleared when a child taps “Done, next player,” when the tab is closed, or after 45 minutes without play.
  • Home mode (turned on by a grown-up): streaks, prizes, the Gallery, help requests, practice results, and the grown-up PIN are saved in this browser on this device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. A grown-up can erase them at any time with “Turn saving off” in the Grown-ups area, or by clearing the browser’s site data.

What we receive

Nothing about you or your child. The games, fonts, and pictures load from our own website. Like any website, our hosting provider handles the basic technical requests needed to deliver the pages; we don’t use that information to identify, track, or contact anyone.

Read-aloud

The 🔊 read-aloud buttons use the voice built into your device. No recordings are made, and Starverse never uses the microphone.

Children

Starverse is made for children in Grades 2 and 5. Because it collects no personal information, no child needs to give us a name, an email, or any other details to play.

Changes

If we ever add something that would collect information, such as accounts, we will update this page first and ask for a parent’s permission before collecting anything from a child.

Who we are

Starverse Learning is a product of NovaCore Technologies LLC, its parent company, which owns and operates this website.
